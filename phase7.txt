Phase 7 — Career Network & Opportunity Platform

Goal:
Turn the university ecosystem into a career discovery platform where students can discover internships, jobs, scholarships, competitions, research opportunities, and organizations—while their existing portfolio becomes their professional profile.

The key principle:

The portfolio remains the student's source of truth. Opportunities connect to the portfolio; they don't replace it.

34. Trust & Safety

This phase requires serious abuse prevention.

We need:

Organization verification
Reporting
Blocking
Rate limits
Anti-spam
Moderation
Suspicious activity detection
Account recovery
Secure authentication
Audit logs

Students are potentially vulnerable users in a career marketplace, so trust should be a core product feature, not an afterthought.

35. What Phase 7 Does NOT Include

Don't immediately turn this into:

❌ LinkedIn clone
❌ Social feed
❌ Influencer platform
❌ Public likes
❌ Followers
❌ Viral content
❌ Generic messaging platform
❌ Automated mass applications
❌ AI applying to jobs automatically

The product should remain focused on:

Student identity + portfolio + career opportunities.
