Phase 10 — Global Student Career Infrastructure

If we're going to continue the roadmap, Phase 10 should be the point where the platform moves from a product into infrastructure.

Phase 9 lets other developers and services build around your platform.

Phase 10 makes the platform capable of operating as a global student professional ecosystem.

The core idea:

A student's professional identity should work across universities, countries, organizations, and career stages.

1. Global Student Identity

The student's profile should no longer be tied primarily to one university.

For example:

Crystal Santos

├── University A
└── University B

├── Internship

├── Project B

Achievements

A student can change universities, graduate, move countries, or start working without losing their identity.
